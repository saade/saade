# Hi, I'm Saade

I'm Guilherme Saade, a backend engineer from Jaraguá do Sul, Brazil. I'm the CTO and co-founder of [GovDigital](https://www.govdigital.app), and I'm on the [Filament](https://filamentphp.com/team) core team, where I look after plugins.

I've been coding since I was 11. My mom was a teacher and wanted a website for her school, so I built one with Blogger. The love for PHP came later, from a book about it that my father had.

Most of what I publish here started as something we needed at work.

## What I do

At GovDigital, we build the software that Brazilian municipalities use to offer public services online. It's a Laravel and Filament platform, and I still write a good part of it.

At Filament, I review the plugins submitted to [filamentphp.com/plugins](https://filamentphp.com/plugins), help their authors, and contribute to the framework.
